Privacy Policy — Forest Strike

Last updated: 14 July 2026

Forest Strike ("the Game") is developed by HA Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Game handles and why. By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect ourselves

None. We do not operate any server and we do not collect, store or transmit any personal information about you. We have no accounts, no login, and no way to identify you.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally on your device only (coins, unlocked daggers, best stage, chosen language). This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

3. Advertising (third party)

The Game shows ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To serve ads, Unity Ads may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID);
  • device information (model, operating system, language, country);
  • coarse, non-precise usage data related to the ads shown.

We never receive this data ourselves. It is handled by Unity under their own policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the ad network, please contact us and we will help you raise it with Unity.

5. Permissions

The Game requests internet access (to load ads) and vibration (for haptic feedback). It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. To remove all locally stored progress, simply uninstall the Game. For data held by Unity Ads, please use the rights described in Unity's policy linked above.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with an updated date at the top.
