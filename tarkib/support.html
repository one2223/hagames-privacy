What do the results mean?

  • Halal certificate found — the product's data mentions a halal certificate. Check the mark on the pack too.
  • No doubtful ingredients found — nothing in the ingredient list needs attention.
  • Needs checking — an ingredient can come from a plant, a synthetic or an animal source (for example E471, glycerin, or gelatine with no source stated). Only the maker can say which one was used.
  • Contains … — the list names something that needs attention, such as pork gelatin, alcohol, carmine (E120) or shellac (E904).
  • Not enough information — the ingredient list is missing or too short to check.

Every finding shows its reason. The E-codes tab lists each code with what it is made from and how it is written on packs.

Does Tarkib say whether a product is halal?

No. Tarkib gives a finding, not a fatwa: it tells you what was found in the ingredients on the pack. Notes follow the Hanafi school; the app does not offer a choice of school. If in doubt, ask the maker or a scholar.

Which codes can I scan?

Barcodes (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC), DataMatrix and GS1 QR codes. If the camera can't read a code, tap Enter code and type the 8 to 14 digits under the barcode.

The product isn't in the database

Tap Photograph the ingredients or Type it in, check the text and tap Check. The result is worked out on your phone. For a product with a code, the checked list is sent to us; after review it is added to the database so that the next person sees it straight away.

Reading the ingredients from a photo

  • Photograph only the ingredient list, straight on and in good light. You can also choose a picture From photos.
  • The text is read on your iPhone. AI then tidies it into a list of ingredients: 3 times a day free, 30 a day with Premium. When the limit is used up, you can still correct the text yourself.
  • Always look over the text before checking: a misread letter can change the result.
  • The photo is not kept and never leaves your phone.

The ingredients or the result look wrong

On the result screen tap Report a mistake, choose what is wrong and add a comment if you like. We check every report. Recipes change: compare with the list on the pack in your hand.

Does it work without internet?

The E-code table, your history and checking text you type work offline. Looking up a code needs internet; a code scanned offline is saved in History and checked automatically when you are back online.

How does "Things I avoid" work?

With Premium, open Settings → Things I avoid and add E-codes or words (for example E621, lactose, nuts). When a product contains one of them, its result shows a warning. The list is yours, kept on your phone, and does not change the result itself.

How do I restore or manage my purchase?

  • Restore: Settings → Tarkib Premium → Restore purchase, with the same Apple Account you bought with. Premium is checked on your iPhone.
  • Change or cancel a subscription: Manage subscription on the Premium screen, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Can't afford Premium? Tap I can't afford it on the Premium screen and write to us; we'll send you a free code. Enter it with Redeem a code.
  • Tarkib Premium purchases are personal and are not shared through Family Sharing.

Natijalar nimani bildiradi?

  • Halol sertifikati bor — mahsulot ma'lumotida halol sertifikati ko'rsatilgan. Qadoqdagi belgini ham tekshiring.
  • Shubhali tarkib topilmadi — tarkibda e'tibor talab qiladigan narsa yo'q.
  • Aniqlashtirish kerak — ingredient o'simlik, sintetik yoki hayvon manbasidan bo'lishi mumkin (masalan, E471, glitserin yoki manbasi yozilmagan jelatin). Qaysi biri ishlatilganini faqat ishlab chiqaruvchi ayta oladi.
  • Tarkibida … bor — ro'yxatda e'tibor talab qiladigan narsa bor: masalan, cho'chqa jelatini, spirt, karmin (E120) yoki shellak (E904).
  • Ma'lumot yetarli emas — tarkib yo'q yoki tekshirish uchun juda qisqa.

Har bir topilma sababi bilan ko'rsatiladi. E-kodlar bo'limida har bir kod — nimadan olinishi va qadoqda qanday yozilishi bilan.

Tarkib mahsulot halolmi-yo'qmi deb aytadimi?

Yo'q. Tarkib fatvo emas, topilma beradi: qadoqdagi tarkibda nima topilganini aytadi. Izohlar Hanafiy mazhabi bo'yicha; ilovada mazhab tanlash yo'q. Shubha bo'lsa, ishlab chiqaruvchidan yoki olimdan so'rang.

Qaysi kodlarni skanerlash mumkin?

Shtrix-kodlar (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC), DataMatrix va GS1 QR kodlari. Kamera kodni o'qiy olmasa, Kodni kiritishni bosib, shtrix-kod ostidagi 8–14 ta raqamni yozing.

Mahsulot bazada yo'q

Tarkibni suratga olish yoki Qo'lda yozishni bosing, matnni ko'zdan kechirib Tekshirishni bosing. Natija telefoningizda hisoblanadi. Kodi bor mahsulot uchun tekshirilgan tarkib bizga yuboriladi; ko'rib chiqilgach bazaga qo'shiladi — keyingi odam uni darhol ko'radi.

Tarkibni suratdan o'qitish

  • Faqat tarkib qismini yorug' joyda, to'g'ridan suratga oling. Rasmni Galereyadan ham tanlash mumkin.
  • Matn iPhone'ingizda o'qiladi. So'ng AI uni ingredientlar ro'yxatiga keltiradi: bepul — kuniga 3 marta, Premium bilan — kuniga 30 marta. Limit tugasa ham matnni o'zingiz tuzatib tekshirishingiz mumkin.
  • Tekshirishdan oldin matnni doim ko'zdan kechiring: noto'g'ri o'qilgan bitta harf natijani o'zgartirishi mumkin.
  • Surat saqlanmaydi va telefondan chiqmaydi.

Tarkib yoki natija noto'g'ri ko'rinyapti

Natija ekranida Xato haqida xabar berishni bosing, nima noto'g'riligini tanlang, xohlasangiz izoh qo'shing. Har bir xabarni tekshiramiz. Retseptlar o'zgaradi: qo'lingizdagi qadoqdagi tarkib bilan solishtiring.

Internetsiz ishlaydimi?

E-kodlar jadvali, tarix va qo'lda yozilgan matnni tekshirish internetsiz ishlaydi. Kodni topish uchun internet kerak; internetsiz skanerlangan kod Tarixga yoziladi va internet qaytganda o'zi tekshiriladi.

"Men iste'mol qilmaydiganlar" qanday ishlaydi?

Premium bilan Sozlamalar → Men iste'mol qilmaydiganlarni ochib, tayyor variantlarni (laktoza, glyuten, yong'oq…) belgilang yoki E-kod va so'z qo'shing (masalan, E621, laktoza, yong'oq). Mahsulotda ulardan biri bo'lsa, natijada ogohlantirish chiqadi. Ro'yxat shaxsiy, telefoningizda saqlanadi va natijaning o'zini o'zgartirmaydi.

Xaridni qanday tiklayman yoki boshqaraman?

  • Tiklash: Sozlamalar → Tarkib Premium → Xaridni tiklash, xarid qilgan Apple Account bilan. Premium iPhone'ingizda tekshiriladi.
  • Obunani o'zgartirish yoki bekor qilish: Premium ekranidagi Obunani boshqarish yoki iOS Sozlamalari → ismingiz → Obunalar. Davr tugashidan kamida 24 soat oldin bekor qiling.
  • Pulni qaytarishni Apple ko'rib chiqadi: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • To'lashga imkoningiz yo'qmi? Premium ekranidagi Imkonim yo'qni bosib bizga yozing — bepul kod yuboramiz. Uni Kodni kiritish orqali ishlating.
  • Tarkib Premium xaridlari shaxsiy — Family Sharing orqali ulashilmaydi.
