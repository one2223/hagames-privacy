Tarkib: halal ingredient and E-code scanner

Scan the code on a pack and see what is in the ingredients — with the reason for every finding.

Download on the App Store

For iPhone, iOS 17 or later · English, O'zbekcha, Русский, العربية, Français, Deutsch

Always free

  • Scan barcodes (EAN, UPC) and DataMatrix and GS1 QR codes — food, cosmetics and household products
  • One of five clear results: Halal certificate found · No doubtful ingredients found · Needs checking · Contains … · Not enough information
  • Every E-code and ingredient that needs attention, with what it is made from and why
  • Not in the database? Read the ingredients from a photo or type them in, and the check is done on your phone
  • E-code table that works offline, with how each code is written on packs
  • Calories and the main nutrients, and a history of your last 30 checks

Tarkib Premium (optional)

  • More label readings tidied up by AI (30 a day instead of 3)
  • The full nutrition table, per 100 g and per portion
  • Unlimited history, favourites and search
  • Things I avoid: a warning when a product has an additive or ingredient you chose

Monthly, yearly (with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase, through the App Store.

A finding, not a fatwa

Tarkib tells you what was found in the ingredients on the pack. It does not say whether a product is permitted. Notes follow the Hanafi school. If in doubt, ask the maker or a scholar.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Photos never leave your iPhone, and your history stays on it. Privacy policy

Tarkib: halol skaner — tarkib va E-kodlar

Qadoqdagi kodni skanerlang va tarkibda nima borligini ko'ring — har bir topilma sababi bilan.

App Store'dan yuklab olish

iPhone uchun, iOS 17 va undan yangi · ilova to'liq o'zbek tilida

Doim bepul

  • Shtrix-kod (EAN, UPC) DataMatrix va GS1 QR kodlarini skanerlash — oziq-ovqat, kosmetika va maishiy kimyo
  • Beshta aniq natijadan biri: Halol sertifikati bor · Shubhali tarkib topilmadi · Aniqlashtirish kerak · Tarkibida … bor · Ma'lumot yetarli emas
  • E'tibor talab qiladigan har bir E-kod va ingredient — nimadan olinishi va sababi bilan
  • Bazada yo'qmi? Tarkibni suratdan o'qiting yoki yozing — tekshiruv telefoningizda bajariladi
  • Internetsiz ishlaydigan E-kodlar jadvali — har kod qadoqda qanday yozilishi bilan
  • Kaloriya va asosiy ozuqa moddalari, oxirgi 30 ta tekshiruv tarixi

Tarkib Premium (ixtiyoriy)

  • AI tozalaydigan ko'proq tarkib o'qish (kuniga 3 ta o'rniga 30 ta)
  • To'liq ozuqa jadvali — 100 g va porsiya uchun
  • Cheksiz tarix, sevimlilar va qidiruv
  • Men iste'mol qilmaydiganlar: allergiya yoki parhez uchun — laktoza, glyuten, yong'oq, soya, tuxum, E621 va boshqalar bo'lsa ogohlantiradi
