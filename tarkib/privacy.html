Privacy Policy (English)

Tarkib ("the app") is an iPhone app made by Islombek Yadgarov, an independent developer (H-A Studio, hagames.uz). This policy explains what happens to information when you use Tarkib.

In short

  • No account and no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Photos never leave your iPhone. Barcodes and labels are read on the phone.
  • Your history, favourites and "Things I avoid" list stay on your iPhone.
  • To look a product up, the app sends its code to our server. Ingredient lists you check for a product with a code are sent to us and, after review, shared with other users.
  • Text read from a photo is tidied up by an AI service (Anthropic). We do not keep that text.

1. Information kept on your iPhone

Tarkib saves on your iPhone: your settings (language, haptics); the history of your checks (product codes, the ingredient text, names you typed, results and dates) and favourites; your "Things I avoid" list; your Premium status as last confirmed by the App Store; and a random install number created on first launch. This stays in the app's private storage and is not sent to us, except as described in section 3. Deleting a history item, or the app, removes it.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. Apple handles those backups; we cannot access them.

2. Camera and photos

The camera is used to read barcodes and the ingredient list on a pack. If you choose a picture from your library, iOS gives Tarkib only that picture. Barcodes and text are recognised on your iPhone by Apple's built-in Vision technology. Photos and camera frames are never saved by the app and never leave your iPhone.

3. Information sent to our server

Tarkib talks only to our own server, api.hagames.uz. Every request carries the random install number, the app version and the platform. The install number is not linked to your name, Apple Account or device identifiers; we use it to limit how often one device can make requests, and store it only in a hashed (one-way) form.

  • Product lookup. When you scan or type a code, the app sends that code. Our server answers from its own product database or asks Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts for it — only the code goes to them, from our server, not from your iPhone. The result is worked out on your iPhone.
  • Ingredient lists you check. When you check an ingredient list for a product that has a code, the app sends the code, the text, the product type, the name you typed (if any) and whether the text came from a photo. We review it; once approved, the product's ingredients and name become part of the shared database that every user sees, without anything that identifies you. Please do not type personal information into these fields. Text checked without a code is not sent.
  • AI tidy-up of label text. When you read a label from a photo, the recognised text (not the photo) and the product type are sent to our server, which asks Anthropic's Claude AI to tidy it into a list of ingredients and return it. We do not store the text; we record only the number of requests and their cost, with the hashed install number, to apply daily limits. Anthropic acts as our service provider and, under its commercial terms, does not use this text to train its models. The AI never decides the result.
  • Report a mistake. The code, the reason you chose and your comment, if you write one.
  • Purchases. After a purchase, the app sends Apple's signed transaction to our server. It contains the product, dates and transaction numbers assigned by Apple — not your name, email address or Apple Account. We use it for our records and to apply the Premium AI limit. Premium itself is unlocked on your iPhone by Apple's StoreKit and works even if our server is unavailable.

Our server is operated by us on a rented virtual server. Like any web server it keeps standard access logs (such as IP address, time and request) for a short time, for security. We keep submissions and reports as long as needed to maintain the product database.

4. Purchases

Tarkib Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple may also send our server standard notices about subscription events, such as a renewal or a refund. Apple's own processing is described in Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. When you write to us

If you email us (for example from "Contact us" or "I can't afford it" in the app), we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply to you and keep it only as long as needed for that. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail).

6. This website

The hagames.uz/tarkib pages use no cookies, analytics or trackers. The web server may keep standard access logs for a short time, for security.

7. Children

Tarkib is suitable for all ages. It does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information.

8. Your rights

Because what the app sends is not linked to you, we usually cannot find "your" data. If you sent an ingredient list or a comment that you want removed, write to us with the product code and we will delete it. If you wrote to us by email, you can ask us to show you or delete your messages. If you live in the EU or the UK, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version of Tarkib sends more data off your iPhone, we will say so here and in the App Store privacy label before that version is released.

Maxfiylik siyosati (O'zbekcha)

Tarkib ("ilova") — mustaqil dasturchi Islombek Yadgarov (H-A Studio, hagames.uz) yaratgan iPhone ilovasi. Bu siyosat Tarkib'dan foydalanganingizda ma'lumot bilan nima bo'lishini tushuntiradi.

Qisqacha

  • Akkaunt va ro'yxatdan o'tish yo'q; reklama, analitika va kuzatuv yo'q.
  • Suratlar iPhone'dan chiqmaydi. Shtrix-kod va tarkib telefonda o'qiladi.
  • Tarix, sevimlilar va "Mendan qochiladiganlar" ro'yxati iPhone'ingizda qoladi.
  • Mahsulotni topish uchun ilova uning kodini serverimizga yuboradi. Kodli mahsulot uchun tekshirgan tarkibingiz bizga yuboriladi va ko'rib chiqilgach, boshqa foydalanuvchilarga ham ko'rinadi.
  • Suratdan o'qilgan matnni AI xizmati (Anthropic) tartibga soladi. Biz bu matnni saqlamaymiz.

1. iPhone'ingizda saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

Tarkib iPhone'ingizda quyidagilarni saqlaydi: sozlamalar (til, tebranish); tekshiruvlar tarixi (mahsulot kodlari, tarkib matni, siz yozgan nomlar, natijalar va sanalar) va sevimlilar; "Mendan qochiladiganlar" ro'yxati; App Store oxirgi marta tasdiqlagan Premium holati; birinchi ishga tushirishda yaratiladigan tasodifiy o'rnatish raqami. Bular ilovaning yopiq xotirasida qoladi va 3-bo'limda yozilganidan boshqa hech narsa bizga yuborilmaydi. Tarixdagi yozuvni yoki ilovani o'chirsangiz, ular ham o'chadi.

iCloud zaxira nusxasi yoki kompyuterga zaxira nusxa olishdan foydalansangiz, iOS ilova ma'lumotlarini shu nusxaga qo'shishi mumkin. Bu nusxalarni Apple boshqaradi; biz ularga kira olmaymiz.

2. Kamera va suratlar

Kamera shtrix-kodni va qadoqdagi tarkibni o'qish uchun ishlatiladi. Galereyadan rasm tanlasangiz, iOS Tarkib'ga faqat o'sha rasmni beradi. Kod va matn iPhone'ingizda Apple'ning o'rnatilgan Vision texnologiyasi bilan aniqlanadi. Suratlar va kamera kadrlari ilovada saqlanmaydi va iPhone'dan hech qachon chiqmaydi.

3. Serverimizga yuboriladigan ma'lumotlar

Tarkib faqat o'zimizning serverimiz — api.hagames.uz bilan bog'lanadi. Har bir so'rovda tasodifiy o'rnatish raqami, ilova versiyasi va platforma bo'ladi. O'rnatish raqami ismingiz, Apple Account yoki qurilma identifikatorlari bilan bog'lanmagan; undan bitta qurilma qancha so'rov yuborishini cheklash uchun foydalanamiz va uni faqat xeshlangan (qaytarib bo'lmaydigan) shaklda saqlaymiz.

  • Mahsulotni topish. Kodni skanerlasangiz yoki yozsangiz, ilova shu kodni yuboradi. Serverimiz o'z bazasidan javob beradi yoki Open Food Facts va Open Beauty Facts'dan so'raydi — ularga faqat kod, iPhone'ingizdan emas, serverimizdan boradi. Natija iPhone'ingizda hisoblanadi.
  • Siz tekshirgan tarkib. Kodi bor mahsulot tarkibini tekshirsangiz, ilova kodni, matnni, mahsulot turini, siz yozgan nomni (bo'lsa) va matn suratdan olinganmi — shuni yuboradi. Biz uni ko'rib chiqamiz; tasdiqlangach, mahsulot tarkibi va nomi hamma foydalanuvchi ko'radigan umumiy bazaga qo'shiladi — sizni aniqlaydigan hech narsasiz. Bu maydonlarga shaxsiy ma'lumot yozmang. Kodsiz tekshirilgan matn yuborilmaydi.
  • Tarkib matnini AI bilan tozalash. Tarkibni suratdan o'qitganingizda aniqlangan matn (surat emas) va mahsulot turi serverimizga yuboriladi; server Anthropic kompaniyasining Claude AI'sidan uni ingredientlar ro'yxatiga keltirib qaytarishni so'raydi. Biz matnni saqlamaymiz; kunlik limit uchun faqat so'rovlar soni va narxini xeshlangan o'rnatish raqami bilan yozib boramiz. Anthropic bizning xizmat ko'rsatuvchimiz sifatida ishlaydi va tijoriy shartlariga ko'ra bu matndan o'z modellarini o'qitishda foydalanmaydi. Natijani AI hech qachon belgilamaydi.
  • Xato haqida xabar. Kod, siz tanlagan sabab va izohingiz (yozsangiz).
  • Xaridlar. Xariddan keyin ilova Apple imzolagan tranzaksiyani serverimizga yuboradi. Unda mahsulot, sanalar va Apple bergan tranzaksiya raqamlari bo'ladi — ismingiz, email manzilingiz yoki Apple Account emas. Undan hisob yuritish va Premium AI limitini qo'llash uchun foydalanamiz. Premium'ning o'zi iPhone'ingizda Apple StoreKit orqali ochiladi va serverimiz ishlamasa ham ishlayveradi.

Serverimizni ijaraga olingan virtual serverda o'zimiz boshqaramiz. Har qanday veb-server kabi u xavfsizlik uchun qisqa muddat standart kirish jurnallarini (IP manzil, vaqt, so'rov kabi) saqlaydi. Yuborilgan tarkiblar va xabarlarni mahsulot bazasini yuritish uchun kerakli muddatgacha saqlaymiz.

4. Xaridlar

Tarkib Premium App Store orqali sotiladi. To'lovni Apple amalga oshiradi; biz ismingiz, email manzilingiz yoki to'lov ma'lumotlaringizni olmaymiz. Apple serverimizga obuna hodisalari — masalan, yangilanish yoki pul qaytarilishi — haqida standart xabarlar ham yuborishi mumkin. Apple'ning o'zi ma'lumotlarni qanday qayta ishlashi uning maxfiylik siyosatida yozilgan: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Bizga yozganingizda

Bizga email yozsangiz (masalan, ilovadagi "Bizga yozing" yoki "Imkonim yo'q" orqali), email manzilingizni va yozganingizni olamiz. Undan faqat sizga javob berish uchun foydalanamiz va shu uchun kerakli muddatgacha saqlaymiz. Pochta qutimiz Google (Gmail) xizmatida.

6. Ushbu sayt

hagames.uz/tarkib sahifalarida cookie, analitika va kuzatuv vositalari yo'q. Veb-server xavfsizlik uchun qisqa muddat standart kirish jurnallarini saqlashi mumkin.

7. Bolalar

Tarkib barcha yoshdagilar uchun mos. U hech kimdan, jumladan bolalardan ham, shaxsiy ma'lumot so'ramaydi.

8. Huquqlaringiz

Ilova yuboradigan ma'lumot siz bilan bog'lanmagani uchun biz odatda "sizning" ma'lumotingizni topa olmaymiz. Yuborgan tarkibingiz yoki izohingiz o'chirilishini istasangiz, mahsulot kodi bilan bizga yozing — o'chiramiz. Bizga email yozgan bo'lsangiz, xatlaringizni ko'rsatishni yoki o'chirishni so'rashingiz mumkin. Yevropa Ittifoqi yoki Buyuk Britaniyada yashasangiz, ma'lumotlarni himoya qilish organiga shikoyat qilishingiz ham mumkin.
