Privacy Policy

Find Top: Hidden Objects (uz.hagames.findtop)
Last updated: 14 August 2026 · Effective: 14 August 2026

This policy explains what information the Find Top mobile game collects, why it is collected, and who it is shared with. It applies to the Android version of the game distributed through Google Play.

The game is developed and operated by HA Games, an individual developer based in Uzbekistan. Throughout this document, "we", "us" and "the developer" refer to HA Games; "the game" and "the app" refer to Find Top: Hidden Objects.

Short version. We do not ask you for your real name, email address, phone number or location. Your game progress is stored on your own device. A nickname you choose and your weekly score are sent to our leaderboard. Advertising and payment are handled by Unity and Google, who collect device identifiers under their own privacy policies.

1. Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone using Android's standard app storage. It never leaves your device unless a section below says otherwise, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

  • Level progress, current level and completed levels
  • In-game coin balance, hints and rewards
  • Achievement progress
  • The nickname you choose for yourself
  • Sound, music and other settings
  • Counters the game uses to decide when to show an advertisement

2. Information sent to our services

Anonymous player account

When the game connects to the leaderboard, it signs you in anonymously through Unity Gaming Services Authentication. This creates a random player identifier that is tied to your app installation. It is not linked to your Google account, your email address or your real identity, and we cannot use it to identify you personally.

Leaderboard

If you take part in the weekly leaderboard, the game sends your chosen nickname and your weekly score to Unity Leaderboards, where they are stored so that scores can be ranked and shown to other players.

The nickname is entirely your choice. If you do not enter one, the game generates a random name such as "Player1502". Please do not use your real name, email address, phone number or any other personal detail as your nickname, because nicknames are visible to other players in the leaderboard.

Parts of the game are provided by external services. When those parts are active, the service collects data directly under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your personal data from them.

Payments

All purchases go through Google Play. We never see and never store your card number, bank details or billing address. We only receive confirmation from Google that a purchase succeeded, so that the game can unlock what you bought.

Advertising identifier

Advertisements may use your device's advertising ID to limit how often you see the same advertisement and to make advertisements more relevant. You can reset or delete this ID at any time in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it does not stop advertisements from appearing; it makes them less personalised.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your real name, email address or phone number inside the game.
  • We do not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • We do not collect GPS or precise location data.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not build advertising profiles ourselves.

5. Permissions the app requests

The game requests internet access, which Android needs in order to load advertisements and to reach the leaderboard. It does not request any permission classified by Android as dangerous. The levels themselves can be played without an internet connection.

6. Children

Find Top is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

7. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data through Android settings.
  • Leaderboard scores: weekly scores reset on a weekly cycle. Your nickname and anonymous player ID remain until you ask us to delete them.
  • Third-party services: retention is governed by the policies of Unity and Google, linked in the table above.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the data held about you, to object to certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. This includes rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

To exercise any of these rights, write to one@zoomlock.uz. We will respond within 30 days. Because leaderboard accounts are anonymous, please include the nickname shown in your game so that we can locate the correct record.

Deleting your data yourself

  • Local progress: uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Find Top → Storage → Clear data.
  • Leaderboard entry: email us and we will remove your nickname and score from the board.

9. Data security

Data sent between the game and our service providers travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so while we take reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers

Unity and Google operate servers in a number of countries, which means your data may be processed outside Uzbekistan, including in the European Union and the United States. These transfers are governed by the privacy policies and safeguards of those providers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes or when the law requires it. The date at the top of this page always shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be announced in the app's Google Play listing. Continuing to use the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
