How are prayer times calculated, and how accurate are they?

Ziyo calculates the times on your iPhone, offline, with standard astronomical formulas. It picks the method used in your country automatically — for example Uzbekistan (Hanafi: Fajr 15.5°, Isha 15°, sunrise shown 5 minutes early, Maghrib +3 minutes), Kazakhstan, Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, ISNA, Türkiye, France (12°) or Russia (DUM RF); 15 methods in all. If Ziyo cannot verify the official method for your country, it asks you once to check it.

Calculated times can differ by a minute or two from a printed timetable or your mosque's, because mosques often add their own precaution minutes. Check that the right place is chosen. In very northern or southern places, where the sun does not always rise or set, times are estimated and Ziyo shows a note.

How do I change the method, Asr or a single prayer's time?

Open Settings (the gear at the top of Today or More) → Calculation. There you can choose the Method, the Asr time (Hanafi or standard), the Hijri date adjustment and Adjust minutes for each prayer, for example to match your mosque. Reset to defaults brings back your country's settings. To change the place: Settings → Location.

My reminders don't arrive

  1. In Ziyo: Settings → Reminders — each prayer should be on Sound or Silent, not Off. If Ziyo shows "Notifications are off", tap Turn on.
  2. In the iOS Settings app: Notifications → Ziyo — Allow Notifications on, with Lock Screen and Banners.
  3. Focus: either add Ziyo to the allowed apps of your Focus, or keep Time Sensitive on — in Ziyo (Settings → Reminders) and in iOS Settings → Notifications → Ziyo.
  4. Silent mode mutes the sound; the notification still appears. If Ziyo is in a Scheduled Summary, its reminders are delayed — take it out of the summary.
  5. Open Ziyo at least once a week. iOS limits how many reminders an app can schedule, so Ziyo plans about a week ahead and refreshes each time you open it. If you haven't opened it for a while, a reminder asks you to.
  6. After travel or a time zone change, open Ziyo once so the reminders are planned for the new place.

Why is there no adhan?

Ziyo has no audio by design. Reminders use the standard iPhone notification sound, or no sound if you choose Silent.

How do I add widgets?

Open Ziyo once first. Then touch and hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → search for Ziyo. For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize.

  • Free: Next prayer (Home Screen and Lock Screen) and Name of the day.
  • Premium: Today (tap a prayer to mark it) and All times.

Widgets keep about a month of prayer times. If a widget says "Open Ziyo to update the prayer times", open the app once.

How do I get the countdown on the Lock Screen?

Turn on Settings → Reminders → Countdown on the Lock Screen (iOS 17 or later). It shows the next prayer — and iftar in Ramadan — on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, without sound. Live Activities must be allowed in iOS Settings → Ziyo. iOS ends a Live Activity after several hours without an update; opening Ziyo starts it again.

How do I restore or manage my purchase?

  • Restore: More → Ziyo Premium → Restore (or Settings → Premium → Ziyo Premium), with the same Apple Account you bought with. Premium is checked on your iPhone and also works offline.
  • Change or cancel a subscription: Manage subscription on the Premium screen, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Can't afford Premium? Tap "Can't afford it?" on the Premium screen and write to us; we'll send you a free code. Redeem it in the App Store: your account picture → Redeem Gift Card or Code.

How does Family Sharing work?

Only the Family plan is shared: the organizer and up to 5 family members in the same Apple Family Sharing group get Premium on their own iPhones, with their own Apple Accounts. Each member opens Ziyo; if Premium does not appear, tap Restore. The Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime purchases are personal and are not shared.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

  1. On the old iPhone: Settings → Data → Export my data, and save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or send it by AirDrop.
  2. On the new iPhone: tap Restore from a backup on Ziyo's first screen, or later Settings → Data → Import from a file.

Prayer marks, excused days and tasbeh sessions are added to what is already on the phone; settings, qada, the course, deeds and the journals are replaced by the file's. Premium is not in the file — use Restore. Keep the file private: it contains your prayer and qada records.

iCloud, iPad, Apple Watch?

Ziyo is an iPhone app. It doesn't sync through iCloud at the moment — use export and import to move your data.

Namoz vaqtlari qanday hisoblanadi va qanchalik aniq?

Ziyo vaqtlarni iPhone'ingizda, internetsiz, standart astronomik formulalar bilan hisoblaydi. Davlatingizda qo'llanadigan usulni o'zi tanlaydi — masalan, O'zbekiston (Hanafiy: Bomdod 15,5°, Xufton 15°, Quyosh 5 daqiqa oldin ko'rsatiladi, Shom +3 daqiqa), Qozog'iston, Umm al-Qura, Musulmon olami ligasi, ISNA, Turkiya, Fransiya (12°) yoki Rossiya (DUM RF); jami 15 ta usul. Davlatingiz uchun rasmiy usulni tasdiqlay olmasa, Ziyo uni bir marta tekshirib qo'yishingizni so'raydi.

Hisoblangan vaqt bosma taqvim yoki masjidingiz jadvalidan bir-ikki daqiqa farq qilishi mumkin — masjidlar ko'pincha o'zlarining ehtiyot daqiqalarini qo'shadi. Joy to'g'ri tanlanganini tekshiring. Quyosh har doim ham chiqib-botmaydigan juda shimoliy yoki janubiy joylarda vaqtlar taxminan hisoblanadi va Ziyo bu haqda izoh ko'rsatadi.

Usulni, Asrni yoki bitta namoz vaqtini qanday o'zgartiraman?

Sozlamalar (Bugun yoki Yana ekranining yuqorisidagi ⚙) → Hisoblash. U yerda Usul, Asr (Hanafiy yoki standart), Hijriy sanani tuzatish va har namoz uchun Daqiqalarni sozlash bor — masalan, masjidingiz jadvaliga moslash uchun. Standart sozlamalarni tiklash davlatingiz sozlamalarini qaytaradi. Joyni o'zgartirish: Sozlamalar → Joylashuv.

Eslatmalar kelmayapti

  1. Ziyo'da: Sozlamalar → Eslatmalar — har namoz Ovoz bilan yoki Ovozsiz bo'lsin, O'chiq emas. Ziyo "Bildirishnomalar o'chiq" deb ko'rsatsa, Yoqishni bosing.
  2. iOS Sozlamalarida: Bildirishnomalar → Ziyo — bildirishnomalarga ruxsat yoqilgan, qulf ekrani va bannerlar bilan.
  3. Fokus rejimi: Ziyo'ni Fokusdagi ruxsat berilgan ilovalarga qo'shing yoki Vaqtga bog'liq (Time Sensitive) yoqilgan bo'lsin — Ziyo'da (Sozlamalar → Eslatmalar) va iOS Sozlamalari → Bildirishnomalar → Ziyoda.
  4. Ovozsiz rejimda ovoz chiqmaydi, lekin bildirishnoma ko'rinadi. Ziyo "Rejalashtirilgan xulosa" (Scheduled Summary) ichida bo'lsa, eslatmalar kechikadi — uni xulosadan chiqaring.
  5. Ziyo'ni haftada kamida bir marta oching. iOS ilova oldindan rejalashtira oladigan eslatmalar sonini cheklaydi, shuning uchun Ziyo taxminan bir hafta oldinga rejalaydi va har ochilganda yangilaydi. Uzoq ochilmasa, ilova o'zi eslatadi.
  6. Safardan yoki vaqt mintaqasi o'zgargandan keyin Ziyo'ni bir marta oching — eslatmalar yangi joyga moslab rejalanadi.

Nega azon yo'q?

Ziyo'da ataylab hech qanday audio yo'q. Eslatmalar iPhone'ning oddiy bildirishnoma ovozi bilan keladi, "Ovozsiz" tanlansa — ovozsiz.

Vidjetlarni qanday qo'shaman?

Avval Ziyo'ni bir marta oching. Keyin uy ekranini bosib turing → Tahrirlash → Vidjet qo'shish → Ziyo'ni qidiring. Qulf ekrani uchun: qulf ekranini bosib turing → Sozlash.

  • Bepul: Keyingi namoz (uy ekrani va qulf ekrani) va Kun ismi.
  • Premium: Bugun (namozni bosib belgilaysiz) va Barcha vaqtlar.

Vidjetlar taxminan bir oylik vaqtlarni saqlaydi. Vidjetda "Namoz vaqtlarini yangilash uchun Ziyo'ni oching" yozuvi chiqsa, ilovani bir marta oching.

Qulf ekranidagi teskari sanoqni qanday yoqaman?

Sozlamalar → Eslatmalar → Qulf ekranida teskari sanoqni yoqing (iOS 17 va undan yangi). U keyingi namozni — Ramazonda iftorni ham — qulf ekranida va Dynamic Island'da ovozsiz ko'rsatadi. iOS Sozlamalari → Ziyoda Live Activities ruxsat etilgan bo'lishi kerak. iOS yangilanmagan Live Activity'ni bir necha soatdan keyin yopadi; Ziyo'ni ochsangiz, qaytadan boshlanadi.

Xaridni qanday tiklayman yoki boshqaraman?

  • Tiklash: Yana → Ziyo Premium → Tiklash (yoki Sozlamalar → Premium → Ziyo Premium), xarid qilgan Apple Account bilan. Premium iPhone'ingizda tekshiriladi va internetsiz ham ishlaydi.
  • Obunani o'zgartirish yoki bekor qilish: Premium ekranidagi Obunani boshqarish yoki iOS Sozlamalari → ismingiz → Obunalar. Davr tugashidan kamida 24 soat oldin bekor qiling.
  • Pulni qaytarishni Apple ko'rib chiqadi: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • To'lashga imkoningiz yo'qmi? Premium ekranidagi "To'lashga imkonim yo'q"ni bosib bizga yozing — bepul kod yuboramiz. Kodni App Store'da kiriting: profil rasmingiz → "Sovg'a kartasi yoki kodni ishlatish" (Redeem Gift Card or Code).

Oila bilan ulashish (Family Sharing) qanday ishlaydi?

Faqat Oilaviy reja ulashiladi: tashkilotchi va Apple Family Sharing guruhidagi 5 tagacha oila a'zosi Premium'ni o'z iPhone'larida, o'z Apple Account'lari bilan oladi. Har bir a'zo Ziyo'ni ochadi; Premium ko'rinmasa, Tiklashni bosadi. Oylik, yillik va umrbod xaridlar shaxsiy — ulashilmaydi.

Ma'lumotlarimni yangi iPhone'ga qanday o'tkazaman?

  1. Eski iPhone'da: Sozlamalar → Ma'lumotlar → Ma'lumotlarimni eksport qilish, faylni Fayllar ilovasiga, iCloud Drive'ga saqlang yoki AirDrop bilan yuboring.
  2. Yangi iPhone'da: Ziyo'ning birinchi ekranida Zaxiradan tiklashni bosing yoki keyinroq Sozlamalar → Ma'lumotlar → Fayldan import qilish.

Namoz belgilari, uzrli kunlar va tasbehlar telefondagilarga qo'shiladi; sozlamalar, qazo, kurs, amallar va kundaliklar fayldagisi bilan almashtiriladi. Premium faylda bo'lmaydi — "Tiklash"dan foydalaning. Faylni ehtiyot qiling: unda namoz va qazo yozuvlaringiz bor.

iCloud, iPad, Apple Watch?

Ziyo — iPhone ilovasi. Hozircha iCloud orqali sinxronlanmaydi — ma'lumotlarni o'tkazish uchun eksport va importdan foydalaning.
