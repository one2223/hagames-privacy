Privacy Policy (English)

Ziyo ("the app") is an iPhone app made by Islombek Yadgarov, an independent developer (H-A Studio, hagames.uz). This policy explains what happens to information when you use Ziyo.

In short

  • Ziyo has no account and no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything you keep in Ziyo stays on your iPhone. We do not receive it.
  • Your location is used only on your iPhone, to calculate prayer times and the Qibla.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple.

1. Information kept on your iPhone

Ziyo saves on your iPhone: your settings (language, place, calculation method, reminders, appearance); prayer marks and excused days; qada counts and plans, and — unless you choose "Keep only the result" — the answers you gave the qada calculator (such as dates, age and the optional hayd and nifas days); the 99 Names course progress and favourite Names; your daily deeds and their marks; the Ramadan fasting journal; tasbeh sessions; the numbers you type into the zakat calculator; and your Premium status as last confirmed by the App Store.

This information stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone and is shared only with Ziyo's own widgets, Live Activity and Siri shortcuts on the same iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Some of it relates to religious practice; because it never leaves your device, we do not process it.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. Apple handles those backups; we cannot access them.

You can delete this information at any time: in the app (for example "Delete from this phone" in Qada), or by deleting the app, which removes all of it.

2. Location

Location is optional. If you tap "Use my current location", iOS asks for permission while using the app; Ziyo never asks for "Always" access. Ziyo uses an approximate location to find your place and calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and, while the Qibla screen is open, to point the compass to true north. If you chose your current location, Ziyo checks it quietly when you open the app to notice travel and offer the times for the new city. Your location is processed on your iPhone and saved there as your place. It is never sent to us or to third parties.

Instead, you can choose a city from the list built into the app or enter coordinates. You can turn location access off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

3. Notifications, widgets and Live Activity

Reminders are local notifications that Ziyo schedules on your iPhone. Ziyo does not use push notifications and has no notification server. The Lock Screen countdown (Live Activity) and the widgets are also updated only on your iPhone.

4. Purchases

Ziyo Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details. Ziyo checks what you have bought with Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone. The app does not send your purchases to us.

Apple gives us sales reports and may send our server (api.hagames.uz) standard notices about subscription events, such as a renewal or a refund. These contain the product, price, dates, the App Store country and a transaction number assigned by Apple — not your name, email address or Apple Account. We use them only for our accounting, refunds and to understand how Premium sells. Apple's own processing is described in Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. When you write to us

If you email us (for example from "Write to us" or "Can't afford it?" in the app), we receive your email address and what you write; the app adds only its version number at the end of the message. For a free Premium code we ask only for your App Store country. We use your email only to reply to you and keep it only as long as needed for that. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail).

6. Export files

"Export my data" creates one file with your Ziyo data. It goes only where you send or save it (for example the Files app or AirDrop). Please keep it private: it contains your prayer and qada records.

7. This website

The hagames.uz/ziyo pages use no cookies, analytics or trackers. The web server may keep standard access logs (such as IP address, time and page) for a short time, for security.

8. Children

Ziyo is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Because the app does not send your data to us, we hold no information about you from the app. If you wrote to us by email, you can ask us to show you or delete your messages. If you live in the EU or the UK, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version of Ziyo ever sends any data off your iPhone, we will say so here and in the App Store privacy label before that version is released.

Maxfiylik siyosati (O'zbekcha)

Ziyo ("ilova") — mustaqil dasturchi Islombek Yadgarov (H-A Studio, hagames.uz) yaratgan iPhone ilovasi. Bu siyosat Ziyo'dan foydalanganingizda ma'lumot bilan nima bo'lishini tushuntiradi.

Qisqacha

  • Ziyo'da akkaunt va ro'yxatdan o'tish yo'q; reklama, analitika va kuzatuv yo'q.
  • Ziyo'da saqlaganingiz hamma narsa iPhone'ingizda qoladi. Biz uni olmaymiz.
  • Joylashuvingiz faqat iPhone'ingizda, namoz vaqtlari va qiblani hisoblash uchun ishlatiladi.
  • Xaridlarni Apple amalga oshiradi.

1. iPhone'ingizda saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

Ziyo iPhone'ingizda quyidagilarni saqlaydi: sozlamalar (til, joy, hisoblash usuli, eslatmalar, ko'rinish); namoz belgilari va uzrli kunlar; qazo sonlari va rejalari, shuningdek — "Faqat natijani saqlash" tanlanmagan bo'lsa — qazo hisoblagichiga bergan javoblaringiz (sanalar, yosh va ixtiyoriy hayz va nifos kunlari kabi); 99 ism kursidagi natijalar va saralangan ismlar; kunlik amallaringiz va ularning belgilari; Ramazon ro'za kundaligi; tasbehlar; zakot hisoblagichiga yozgan raqamlaringiz; App Store oxirgi marta tasdiqlagan Premium holati.

Bu ma'lumotlar iPhone'dagi ilovaning yopiq xotirasida qoladi va faqat shu iPhone'dagi Ziyo vidjetlari, Live Activity va Siri buyruqlari bilan bo'lishiladi. Ular bizga ham, boshqa hech kimga ham yuborilmaydi. Ularning bir qismi diniy amallarga oid; ular qurilmangizdan chiqmagani uchun biz ularni qayta ishlamaymiz.

iCloud zaxira nusxasi yoki kompyuterga zaxira nusxa olishdan foydalansangiz, iOS ilova ma'lumotlarini shu nusxaga qo'shishi mumkin. Bu nusxalarni Apple boshqaradi; biz ularga kira olmaymiz.

Bu ma'lumotlarni istalgan vaqtda o'chirishingiz mumkin: ilovaning o'zida (masalan, Qazo bo'limidagi "Telefondan o'chirish") yoki ilovani o'chirib — shunda hammasi o'chadi.

2. Joylashuv

Joylashuv ixtiyoriy. "Joriy joylashuvimdan foydalanish"ni bossangiz, iOS ilovadan foydalanish paytida ruxsat so'raydi; Ziyo hech qachon "Har doim" ruxsatini so'ramaydi. Ziyo taxminiy joylashuvdan joyingizni topish, namoz vaqtlari va qibla yo'nalishini hisoblash uchun, Qibla ekrani ochiq turganda esa kompasni haqiqiy shimolga to'g'rilash uchun foydalanadi. Joriy joylashuvni tanlagan bo'lsangiz, Ziyo ochilganda safarni sezish va yangi shahar vaqtlarini taklif qilish uchun uni jimgina tekshiradi. Joylashuvingiz iPhone'ingizda qayta ishlanadi va o'sha yerda joyingiz sifatida saqlanadi. U hech qachon bizga yoki uchinchi tomonga yuborilmaydi.

Buning o'rniga ilova ichidagi ro'yxatdan shahar tanlashingiz yoki koordinatalarni kiritishingiz mumkin. Joylashuv ruxsatini istalgan vaqtda iOS Sozlamalarida o'chirib qo'yish mumkin.

3. Bildirishnomalar, vidjetlar va Live Activity

Eslatmalar — Ziyo iPhone'ingizda rejalashtiradigan mahalliy bildirishnomalar. Ziyo push-bildirishnomalardan foydalanmaydi va bildirishnoma serveri yo'q. Qulf ekranidagi teskari sanoq (Live Activity) va vidjetlar ham faqat iPhone'ingizda yangilanadi.

4. Xaridlar

Ziyo Premium App Store orqali sotiladi. To'lovni Apple amalga oshiradi; biz ismingiz, email manzilingiz, Apple Account yoki to'lov ma'lumotlaringizni olmaymiz. Ziyo nimani sotib olganingizni iPhone'ingizda Apple'ning StoreKit tizimi orqali tekshiradi. Ilova xaridlaringizni bizga yubormaydi.

Apple bizga sotuv hisobotlarini beradi va serverimizga (api.hagames.uz) obuna hodisalari — masalan, yangilanish yoki pul qaytarilishi — haqida standart xabarlar yuborishi mumkin. Ularda mahsulot, narx, sanalar, App Store mamlakati va Apple bergan tranzaksiya raqami bo'ladi — ismingiz, email manzilingiz yoki Apple Account emas. Biz ulardan faqat hisob-kitob, pulni qaytarish va Premium qanday sotilayotganini tushunish uchun foydalanamiz. Apple'ning o'zi ma'lumotlarni qanday qayta ishlashi uning maxfiylik siyosatida yozilgan: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Bizga yozganingizda

Bizga email yozsangiz (masalan, ilovadagi "Bizga yozing" yoki "To'lashga imkonim yo'q" orqali), biz email manzilingizni va yozganingizni olamiz; ilova xat oxiriga faqat o'z versiya raqamini qo'shadi. Bepul Premium kodi uchun faqat App Store mamlakatingizni so'raymiz. Email'ingizdan faqat sizga javob berish uchun foydalanamiz va uni shu uchun kerakli muddatgacha saqlaymiz. Pochta qutimiz Google (Gmail) xizmatida.

6. Eksport fayllari

"Ma'lumotlarimni eksport qilish" Ziyo ma'lumotlaringiz bilan bitta fayl yaratadi. U faqat siz yuborgan yoki saqlagan joyga boradi (masalan, Fayllar ilovasi yoki AirDrop). Uni ehtiyot qiling: unda namoz va qazo yozuvlaringiz bor.

7. Ushbu sayt

hagames.uz/ziyo sahifalarida cookie, analitika va kuzatuv vositalari yo'q. Veb-server xavfsizlik uchun qisqa muddat standart kirish jurnallarini (IP manzil, vaqt, sahifa kabi) saqlashi mumkin.

8. Bolalar

Ziyo barcha yoshdagilar uchun mos. U hech kimdan, jumladan bolalardan ham, shaxsiy ma'lumot to'plamaydi.

9. Huquqlaringiz

Ilova ma'lumotlaringizni bizga yubormagani uchun ilovadan kelgan siz haqingizdagi hech qanday ma'lumot bizda yo'q. Bizga email yozgan bo'lsangiz, xatlaringizni ko'rsatishni yoki o'chirishni so'rashingiz mumkin. Yevropa Ittifoqi yoki Buyuk Britaniyada yashasangiz, ma'lumotlarni himoya qilish organiga shikoyat qilishingiz ham mumkin.

10. O'zgarishlar

Bu siyosat o'zgarsa, ushbu sahifa va uning kuchga kirish sanasi yangilanadi. Agar Ziyo'ning kelgusi versiyasi biror ma'lumotni iPhone'dan tashqariga yuboradigan bo'lsa, buni shu versiya chiqishidan oldin shu yerda va App Store maxfiylik yorlig'ida yozamiz.
