1. Introduction

HAGames (“we”, “us”, “our”), operating as Mevazor Ikbol, develops and publishes the mobile game Blob Merge: Drop & Merge (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App and its third-party service providers collect, how that information is used and shared, and what choices you have.

By downloading, installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not ask you to create an account with a username or password. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, microphone audio, camera input, precise GPS location, or any government identifier. The App has no chat, no messaging, no friend lists and no social feed.

2.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App saves your gameplay data directly on your device using standard local storage. This includes:

  • Your current board state and high score
  • Which blob tiers you have unlocked
  • Booster counts (for example, remaining Bomb boosters)
  • Sound, music and vibration settings
  • Records of completed in-app purchases (for restoring your entitlements)

This local save data stays on your device. We cannot read it remotely. Deleting or uninstalling the App permanently erases this data, and it cannot be recovered.

2.3 Leaderboard Data

The App includes an optional global leaderboard. When the leaderboard is used, the following happens:

  • Unity Gaming Services (UGS) Authentication signs your device in anonymously and issues a randomly generated player ID. This ID is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any social account, and we cannot use it to identify you personally.
  • Your best score is transmitted to and stored on Unity’s servers, associated with that anonymous player ID, so it can be ranked against other players.
  • The leaderboard displays anonymous player IDs and scores. It does not display any personal information about you.

The leaderboard is the only feature that transmits gameplay data off your device. All other gameplay works fully offline. If you never open the leaderboard and never go online, no score is transmitted.

Reinstalling the App generates a new anonymous player ID, and your previous leaderboard entry can no longer be linked to your device.

2.4 Data Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

When advertising and analytics services are active, the following categories of data may be collected automatically:

  • Device information: model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, screen resolution, and available memory
  • Advertising identifier: your Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • Approximate location: derived from IP address, at country or region level only — never precise GPS coordinates
  • App usage and diagnostics: session length, crash reports, performance data, ad impressions, ad clicks, and reward completions

We use this data to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, diagnose crashes, and improve the App.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Unity Ads and Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies)

The App displays advertisements through Unity Ads and may use Unity Analytics. Unity acts as an independent data controller for the data it collects. Unity may collect device identifiers, IP address, advertising ID and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.

  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Unity Ads opt-out: https://unity3d.com/legal/privacy-policy (see “Your Choices”)

You may see two types of ads:

  • Interstitial ads, shown between game sessions
  • Rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward such as a booster use or a continue

Rewarded ads are always optional. You are never required to watch an ad to continue playing.

3.2 Unity Gaming Services — Authentication and Leaderboards

The global leaderboard is powered by Unity Gaming Services. UGS Authentication creates an anonymous player ID for your device, and UGS Leaderboards stores your submitted best score against that ID. Unity acts as a data processor and, for some diagnostic data, as an independent controller.

  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Unity Gaming Services Terms: https://unity.com/legal/terms-of-service/cloud

If UGS is unreachable — no network, or a service outage — the App continues to run normally and simply shows an empty leaderboard. No gameplay feature is blocked by it.

3.3 Google Play Services and Google Play Billing

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. HAGames never receives, stores or processes your credit card number, bank details or billing address. Google handles all payment data. Please review Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.4 No Other SDKs

The App does not integrate social login, cross-app tracking networks, or data brokers beyond the services named above.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases, which may include booster packs, currency bundles, and an ad-removal purchase. All purchases are:

  • Entirely optional; the full game is playable for free
  • Processed exclusively through Google Play Billing
  • Subject to Google Play’s refund policy

Purchases made by minors should be authorized by a parent or guardian. We strongly recommend that parents enable purchase authentication in the Google Play Store settings. To request a refund, contact Google Play Support; HAGames cannot issue refunds directly for Google Play transactions.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is rated for a general audience and may appeal to children.

We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation including Article 8 (GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).
  • Where a user is identified or age-flagged as a child, advertising is served in a non-personalized, contextual mode only, and behavioral profiling for advertising purposes is disabled.
  • The App contains no chat, no user-generated content, no external social media links inside gameplay, and no unmoderated communication features.
  • The leaderboard uses an anonymous, randomly generated player ID and shows scores only. A child cannot enter a nickname or any free text, so no personal information can be published through it. Under COPPA this identifier is used solely for internal operations — ranking scores — and never for behavioral advertising or profiling.
  • Ads shown to child audiences are filtered to age-appropriate content categories through Unity’s family-compliant ad configuration.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the email address in Section 11. We will delete the information promptly.

6. How We Use Information

We and our service providers use collected information to:

  • Operate the game and save your progress
  • Rank submitted scores on the global leaderboard
  • Deliver, target and measure advertising
  • Process and restore in-app purchases
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and technical exploits
  • Diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance
  • Understand aggregate gameplay trends to improve game design

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

7. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA / UK Users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process personal data on these bases:

  • Consent: for personalized advertising and optional analytics. You may withdraw consent at any time through the in-app privacy settings or your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests: for fraud prevention, security, crash diagnostics and non-personalized advertising.
  • Contractual necessity: to deliver in-app purchases you have bought.

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Opt Out of Personalized Ads

On Android: Settings > Google > Ads — then “Delete advertising ID” or “Opt out of Ads Personalization”. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

8.2 Delete Your Data

Uninstalling the App deletes all locally stored progress and settings permanently.

To have a submitted leaderboard entry removed, email us at one@zoomlock.uz. We will delete the entry associated with your anonymous player ID. Because that ID is the only link we hold, please send your request from the device that made the submission, or include the player ID shown on the leaderboard screen.

8.3 Data Subject Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (EEA, UK, Brazil/LGPD, California/CCPA-CPRA and others), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Because we do not maintain user accounts, we may be unable to identify data belonging to a specific individual; in that case we will direct you to the relevant third-party service provider. Submit requests to the contact address in Section 11.

8.4 California Residents

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA. You will not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

9. Data Retention and Security

Local save data persists on your device until you delete the App. Data held by Unity and Google is retained according to their own published retention schedules.

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data processed by Unity and Google may be transferred to and stored in countries outside your own, including the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards implemented by those providers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
