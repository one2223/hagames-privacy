1. Introduction

HAGames (“we”, “us”, “our”), operating as Mevazor Ikbol, develops and publishes the mobile game Commute Jam (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App, our game server and our third-party service providers collect, how it is used and shared, and what choices you have.

By downloading, installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

The App has no sign-up and no username or password. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, microphone audio, camera input, precise GPS location, or any government identifier. The App has no chat, no messaging and no user-generated content.

2.2 Data Stored on Your Device

Your progress is saved on your device: current level, coins, booster counts, whether you have bought “No Ads”, daily reward status, and sound and vibration settings. We cannot read this data remotely. Deleting the App erases it.

2.3 Data Sent to Our Game Server

The App talks to our own server (api.hagames.uz) only to verify and restore in-app purchases. For that it sends:

  • An install ID — a random identifier the App creates on first launch and keeps in your device’s Keychain. It is not your name, Apple ID or advertising identifier, and we cannot use it to identify you personally.
  • Purchase records — the signed transaction that the App Store gives the App after a purchase (transaction ID, product, purchase date and environment). We check Apple’s signature, deliver the item and keep a record so that a purchase is never delivered twice and “No Ads” can be restored.
  • Technical data — app version, platform, and your IP address, which we use only for security and abuse prevention (rate limiting). Server logs store a one-way hash of the install ID, not the ID itself.

We never receive your payment card, bank details or Apple ID password; Apple handles all payments.

2.4 Data Collected by Third-Party Services

Advertising, analytics and crash reporting services built into the App may collect automatically:

  • Device information: model, operating system version, language, screen size
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): only if you allow tracking when iOS asks (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the IDFA is not available to the App or its partners.
  • Approximate location: country or region derived from your IP address — never precise GPS location
  • Usage and diagnostics: levels played, session length, ad impressions and clicks, rewarded ads watched, crash reports and performance data

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob: interstitial ads between levels, an optional banner, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a reward (for example, a continue or extra coins). Rewarded ads are always optional. Buying “No Ads” removes interstitial and banner ads.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the App asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice later in the App’s settings.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3.2 Google Firebase (analytics, crash reports, remote settings)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how players progress (for example, which levels are too hard), Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes, and Firebase Remote Config to tune game settings without an update.

  • Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3.3 Apple — App Store and Game Center

In-app purchases are processed by the App Store under Apple’s terms. If you are signed in to Game Center, the App may submit your best level to a Game Center leaderboard; Game Center is operated by Apple and shows your Game Center name to other players according to your Game Center settings.

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3.4 No Other SDKs

The App does not include social login, data brokers or other tracking networks beyond the services named above. We do not sell your personal information for money.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional purchases: coin packs, a Starter Pack, and “No Ads” bundles. The whole game can be played for free. Purchases are processed by Apple; refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. When Apple refunds a purchase, our server is notified and the purchased item may be removed.

Parents can require approval for purchases with Apple’s “Ask to Buy” and Screen Time settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. How We Use Information

  • Run the game and save your progress
  • Verify, deliver and restore in-app purchases, and handle refunds
  • Show and measure advertising
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Find and fix crashes, and improve levels and game balance

7. Legal Basis (EEA / UK)

  • Contract: to deliver and restore purchases you made.
  • Consent: for personalised advertising (and tracking, through iOS App Tracking Transparency). You may withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention, crash diagnostics, aggregate analytics and non-personalised ads.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Tracking: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking — turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track” or tracking for Commute Jam.
  • Personalised ads (EEA/UK/Switzerland): change or withdraw your consent at any time with Privacy choices in the App’s settings.
  • US state privacy rights: under some US state laws (for example California), showing personalised ads through Google AdMob may count as “sharing” personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out with Privacy choices in the App’s settings (“Don’t sell or share my data”); you will still see ads, but not personalised ones.
  • Delete local data: delete the App.
  • Delete server data: email us. Because we hold no name or email, please include the Player ID shown in the App’s settings, or the order ID from Apple’s purchase receipt email. We will delete your install record; purchase records that the law requires us to keep for accounting are kept only for that purpose.
  • Other rights: depending on where you live (EEA, UK, California and others), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to processing. We do not sell personal information for money. To exercise these rights, use Privacy choices in the App or email us.

9. Retention, Security and Transfers

Local data stays on your device until you delete the App. Purchase records on our server are kept while needed to deliver and restore purchases and for accounting. Server logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting. Firebase Analytics event data is kept for 14 months. Google and Apple keep other data according to their own policies.

Our server is reached only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data. Our service providers may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change, and material changes will be announced in the App or its App Store listing.
